Booked through us, held to a standard

A map can show you a pin. It cannot check a certificate, hold your deposit, or replace a pilot who breaks down on the way to your field. Found From Above is a booking network, and every search booked here runs under the standards on this page: verified pilots, platform held payment, published refund rules, and reviews that come only from completed bookings. What every pilot here has passed

  • FAA Part 107, checked by usWe verify the pilot's remote pilot certificate before the listing goes live. It is a document check, not a checkbox on a form.
  • Insurance, current and on fileEvery pilot carries liability insurance that we verify. Pilots must report a lapse, and a lapsed policy means no dispatches until it is renewed.
  • Thermal equipment, confirmedA true thermal camera on the aircraft. A spotlight alone is not a thermal search, and it is not listed as one.
  • Recovery only disciplinePilots agree in writing to fly recovery only, under each state's conditions, including the stop rule: a live animal that cannot lawfully be recovered ends the flight.
  • A 15 minute dispatch responseWhen we offer a pilot your search, they commit to answering within 15 minutes during operating hours (5 AM to 11 PM ET), so you are never left wondering whether anyone is coming.
  • Reviews from completed jobs onlyAfter a search, we ask the customer for a review tied to that booking. Reviews live on the pilot's platform profile, and a pilot cannot import praise from elsewhere or delete a fair one.

Why this page exists

The biggest names in this business sell directory listings. Their pilots set their own prices, collect their own payment, and answer to their own standards, and their guarantee, where one exists, caps out below the price of a premium search and never touches your payment at all.

Our protection is structural instead of promotional. Money flows through the platform, so there is always a record and a refund path. Verification happens before a pilot lists, not after a complaint. And the rules on this page are the same rules written into our Terms of Use, so operations has to keep them, not just marketing.

One limit, stated plainly: no honest operator can guarantee your animal is found. We guarantee the standard, the verification, and the process. Anyone who guarantees the recovery is selling you something.

How your booking is protected

Deposit, replacement, and payment

1

Your deposit holds a verified pilot

The $100 deposit is charged only after a pilot accepts your search and confirms the full price and arrival window. If no pilot accepts, you are charged nothing. Before acceptance, the deposit is fully refundable.

2

If your pilot cannot fly, we replace them

Breakdowns, weather, and emergencies happen. If your pilot cannot fly, we rematch your search with another verified pilot in range. If none can take it, the deposit is refunded or rescheduled under our Terms.

3

The platform handles the money

You pay Found From Above, never cash to a stranger in a field. The balance is invoiced after the search, and the pilot is paid by the platform. If something goes wrong, there is a record and a refund path, both in writing.

The refund rules, in short

Before a pilot accepts your search: full refund. After acceptance: the deposit covers dispatch, and if the search never occurs it is refundable or reschedulable. Once the search has been flown: the flight fee is earned, because the pilot's time, travel, and flight are the service. The complete rules are in Section 5 of our Terms of Use.

Questions

Standards and protection FAQ

Do you guarantee you will find my animal?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. A thermal search greatly improves the odds, but the service is the pilot's time, travel, and flight, and the search fee applies even when the animal is not located. What we guarantee is the standard on this page: a verified pilot, a protected deposit, and an honest report of what was searched.

What happens if no pilot accepts my request?

You pay nothing beyond the request itself, which is free. The $100 deposit is only charged after a pilot accepts your search and confirms the price and window. If no pilot can take it, there is no deposit and no charge, and we will tell you plainly and suggest alternatives such as a tracking dog for deer.

What if my pilot has to cancel?

We rematch you with another verified pilot covering your area. If no replacement pilot can take the search, your deposit is refunded or applied to a rescheduled search, under the refund rules in our Terms of Use.

Is my deposit refundable?

Before a pilot accepts, the deposit is fully refundable. After acceptance, the deposit covers dispatch; if the search never occurs, it is refundable or reschedulable. Once the search has been flown, the flight fee is earned. The full rules are in Section 5 of our Terms of Use.

How fast will I hear back after I send a request?

We call back within about 30 minutes between 5 AM and 11 PM ET. Requests that come in overnight get a call by 9 AM ET. The callback is a person from our team confirming the details and the match, not an automated reply.

How do I know a pilot is really verified?

We check the pilot's FAA Part 107 certificate and current insurance ourselves before the listing goes live, and pilots must tell us if their insurance lapses. Reviews on a pilot's profile come only from searches booked and completed through this platform, never from the open internet.

Why not just hire a pilot directly off a map?

You can, and many people do. What changes when you book here: your payment runs through the platform instead of cash to a stranger, the pilot was verified before listing, the deposit and refund rules are written down before anyone flies, and if your pilot cannot fly we work to replace them. That structure is the service.
