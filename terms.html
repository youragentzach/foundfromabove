Drone deer recovery is regulated state by state. We offer deer recovery bookings only in states we have determined allow it. By requesting a deer recovery, you represent that:

4. Requests, bookings, and fees

  • Customers pay the pilot's quoted rate for the search. Some searches include a success fee if the animal is located, as stated in the pilot's quote.
  • Deposits may be required to confirm a booking. Found From Above charges pilots a commission on jobs booked through the platform and may charge customers a booking fee. Any booking fee will be disclosed before you confirm.
  • Payments collected through the platform are processed by Stripe.

5. Cancellations and refunds

  • Once a search flight has been performed, the fee for that flight is earned whether or not the animal is located.
