Lost pet drone search in Georgia

When a dog or cat gets out, the first hours matter more than any other part of the search. A thermal drone covers woods, fields, and neighborhoods from the air, quietly, without pushing a scared pet farther from home. Pet search is not deer recovery: there is no season and no legality gate, and it runs year round across Georgia.

We are building this service Atlanta metro first, because that is where the need showed up: our first customer request came from Marietta. Requests from anywhere in Georgia are welcome, and every request gets a callback within about 30 minutes, 5 AM to 11 PM ET.

How it works

What makes a pet search work

  • Call in the first hoursA fresh trail and a small search circle are worth more than any camera. The pets found fastest are usually the ones searched for the same day they got out.
  • The last seen point runs the searchThe pilot builds the first grid around where your pet was last actually seen, not where it might be headed. A pinned location and a time beat a neighborhood name every time.
  • Permission for launch and landingThe pilot needs the landowner's permission for the ground they take off from and land on. In neighborhoods that is usually your own yard or a neighbor who says yes.
  • Night is an advantageThermal contrast peaks after dark, and a lost pet moves and shows against cooling ground. Many pet searches are flown at night on purpose.
  • Quiet ground confirmationIf the drone finds a heat signature that fits, the pilot guides you in quietly. Nobody chases. A scared pet that is run off can leave the search area entirely.
  • Dogs, cats, and moreMost calls are dogs. Cats, and occasionally other animals, are searched too. Pet search runs year round and is not limited by deer season or deer recovery law.

Questions

Georgia pet search FAQ

How much does a drone search for a lost pet cost in Georgia?

Most Georgia pet searches run $200 to $450, with a standard search of about 2 hours around $300. Your pilot confirms the full price before you book, and a $100 deposit holds the booking only after a pilot accepts your request.

How fast can a pilot get to us?

Send the request as soon as you know your pet is out. We answer requests with a callback within about 30 minutes between 5 AM and 11 PM ET, and overnight requests get a call by 9 AM ET. Many searches can be flown the same day or the same night, and thermal works especially well after dark.

What does the pilot need from me?

The last seen point and time, a recent photo showing your pet's size and markings, and permission for the pilot to take off and land on the property. Sightings after the loss help draw the search circle. The more precise the last seen point, the better the first grid.

Can a thermal drone find a lost cat?

Often, yes. Cats usually hide close to home, under decks, in sheds, in brush, and a thermal camera can spot a warm body in those places without anyone walking through and scaring the cat deeper into cover. Small size and heavy cover make cats harder than dogs, and your pilot will tell you honestly what the ground looks like for your search.

Is it legal to fly a drone to find a lost pet in Georgia?

Yes. Pet search is not hunting, so the deer recovery rules do not apply. The pilot still needs FAA authorization where airspace requires it and the landowner's permission to take off and land, and our pilots hold a Part 107 certificate and insurance.

What if the search does not find my pet?

The search fee still applies, because the service is the pilot's time, travel, and flight. A thermal search covers ground fast and quietly, and a search that finds nothing still rules out the most likely hiding areas while the trail is fresh. No honest operator guarantees a find.
